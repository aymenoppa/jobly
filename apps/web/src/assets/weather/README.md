Weather icons for Snow → Weather (features/snow/SnowWeatherPage.tsx): the sky for now, each hour and
each day.

They are Meteocons by Bas Milius (https://github.com/basmilius/meteocons, meteocons.com), the "fill"
style, under the MIT License in LICENSE here: animated/ from @meteocons/svg 0.1.0 and static/ from
@meteocons/svg-static 0.1.0, unchanged. Only the eleven the page uses are here, not the whole set.
The animated ones move by themselves (SVG animation, no script); the page shows the static ones
wherever there are many at once, and to someone who asked their device for less motion.
