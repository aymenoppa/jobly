google-maps-logo.svg is the Google Maps logo with a dark outline, the one Google gives for use over
imagery, from Google Maps Platform's attribution assets
(https://developers.google.com/static/maps/documentation/images/Google_Maps_Attribution_Assets.zip,
GoogleMaps_Logo_WithDarkOutline/GoogleMaps_Logo_WithDarkOutline.svg, downloaded 2026-11-14). The
file is Google's, byte for byte: its guidance says not to change the logo in any way, to keep its
shape, to show it 16 to 19 px tall with clear space round it (10 px left, right and above, 5 px
below), and to label it "Google Maps". The measuring map shows it at the file's own size, bottom
left, while it shows Google's satellite tiles (features/measure/googleAttribution.ts).
