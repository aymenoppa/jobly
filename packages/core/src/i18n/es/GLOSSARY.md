# Spanish glossary

Spanish as US trade crews speak it (Latin American, not Spain). The app speaks to the crew member
with **tú** ("Marca tu entrada", "Tus visitas"). Short, plain words, as on the English screens; no
exclamation marks. Business content (service names, checklist items, guides, notes, chat) is never
translated.

Read the line in its screen before writing it: the same English can need different Spanish
("Open" a visit is "Abrir"; an "Open" invoice is "Abierta"), which is what `context` is for.
